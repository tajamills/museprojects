# 90 Plug-and-Post Captions

**How to use this pack:** Copy a caption, fill in the [BRACKETS], and post — that's it.
Words in brackets like [BUSINESS NAME], [CITY], [OFFER], and [DAY] are fill-ins for your details.
Swap in your product, service, hours, or offer wherever you see them.
Use one as-is on a busy day, or add a photo and a line of your own when you have time.
